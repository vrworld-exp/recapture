## 1. The plan in 60 seconds

**What we sell.** Restaurants get a QR menu where diners **see the dish in 3D and put it on their table in AR**
before they order. The owner (or our rep) builds it with the ReCapture app in one visit. It costs ₹1,199–2,499 a
month.

**The three big bets:**

1. **The demo sells it, not the ad.** A diner seeing a 3D paneer tikka sitting on the table is the whole pitch.
   Every channel we use exists to put a phone with a live demo in a restaurant owner's hand. That means field reps
   first, and Instagram reels that *show* the AR rather than describe it.
2. **Go deep in 2–3 cities, one area at a time.** A food street with 8 of 30 restaurants on Mirage sells the other
   22 for us. Fifteen restaurants in one area beat 50 spread across a city.
3. **Every table is an ad.** Each live restaurant puts our standee in front of hundreds of diners a week, and some
   of them own or run restaurants. Make it easy to trace a scan back to us, and make it worth an owner's while to
   refer a friend.

**First 90 days:** sign 30 **Founding Restaurants** across 2–3 neighbourhoods. Get 10 of them to share real
numbers (AR views, scans) for case studies. Post 3 reels a week. Spend nothing on ads until a demo-to-signup rate
proves the pitch works.

**12-month target (assumption, revisit at day 90):** 300 paying restaurants across 3 cities, roughly ₹5 lakh
monthly recurring revenue at an average of ₹1,700/month.

### Why us (and not the free QR menu they already have)

| They say | We say |
|---|---|
| "I already have a QR menu / PDF." | A PDF shows a list. Ours shows the actual dish in 3D on the diner's table, and tells you which dishes people looked at. |
| "Zomato has my photos." | Zomato sends diners to other restaurants. Your menu sends them to *your* dishes, with your offers and your WhatsApp. |

### Brand voice rules

- Show, don't claim. A 10-second screen recording beats any adjective.
- Talk money and diners, not technology. Say "customers see the dish", not "photogrammetry" or "GLB".
- Use Hinglish where it's natural on social media. Keep the website and pitch English simple.
- **Never promise what isn't built** (see §11 *Don't promise*).

## 4. The sales motion: field reps + demo (the core engine)

Social media and ads *feed* this. They don't replace it. Restaurant owners in India buy from a person they've met.

### 4.1 The demo kit (every rep carries this)

- A phone with **3 demo menus** loaded: a café, a North Indian restaurant, a dessert shop. Real dishes, fully
  customized (theme, badges, offers, My plate), so the owner sees the best version.
- **5 printed demo standees** that scan to those demo menus.
- **10 blank, pre-minted standees** for same-day activation.
- A one-page leave-behind: three plan prices, the free trial, a QR to book a callback.

### 4.2 The 10-minute pitch (in the restaurant, not on a call)

1. **(1 min) Hook.** "Can I show your customers something in 10 seconds?" Hand them the demo standee. They scan
   it and the dish appears on *their* table in AR.
2. **(2 min) Their dish.** With permission, photograph one of their dishes with Maya AI Capture (6 photos, about a minute).
   It builds while you talk.
3. **(3 min) Money.** Show Analytics and the weekly report on the demo menu: "You'll know which dish people looked
   at most." Then show Offers / happy hour and the WhatsApp order button.
4. **(2 min) Price.** Start the **30-day free trial** today. Taste ₹1,199, Signature ₹1,799 (recommend it), MasterChef ₹2,499.
5. **(2 min) Close.** Activate a standee on the spot: code → owner's phone → publish. "Your menu is live on
   this table right now."

### 4.3 Rep targets and pay (suggested)

| Metric | Target per rep |
|---|---|
| Restaurant visits | 15–20 per day, clustered in one area |
| Demos given (owner/manager actually scanned) | 8 per day |
| Same-day activations (trial started) | 2 per day |
| Trial → paid conversion | ≥ 40% |

**Pay suggestion:** fixed base + incentive per **paid** restaurant (not per activation), plus a second payout if
the restaurant is still paying after 3 months. This rewards quality over standee-dumping.

### 4.4 Area selection ("cluster attack")

Pick **one food street / market / mall food court per city** with 25+ restaurants. Visit all of them in week 1.
Return weekly. Once 5 are live, open with: "Bikanerwala and Café X next door are already on it." Move to the next
cluster only when the current one has 8+ live.

## 5. Acquisition: how restaurant owners find out about us

### 5.1 Social media (main organic channel)

**Platforms, in order:**

| Platform | Role | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** (Reels first) | Main channel. Restaurant owners and diners both live here | 3–4 reels + 3 stories / week |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Repost the same reels, at zero extra cost | Same as Instagram |
| **WhatsApp** (Business + Channel) | Follow-up with leads; share demos; status updates | Daily status, weekly broadcast to leads |
| **LinkedIn** (founder profile) | Credibility with chains, hotel F&B managers, investors | 2 posts / week |
| **Google Business Profile** (for MayasabhaXR) | "QR menu near me" searches; reviews from restaurant owners | Weekly post + every new review answered |
| Facebook | Only via cross-posting from Instagram | — |

**Content pillars (reels):**

1. **"See it before you order" (40%).** A screen recording of a real dish popping up in AR on a real table.
   10–15 seconds, no talking needed, text on screen. *This is the format most likely to go viral.*
2. **Before / after (20%).** A boring laminated menu → the same restaurant's 3D menu. Or a photo → the 3D model spinning.
3. **Owner stories (20%, starts once restaurants are live).** A 30-second clip of the owner: "Paneer tikka views went
   up, so we put it in the spotlight." Use real numbers from the weekly report, with permission.
4. **Behind the scenes (10%).** The rep capturing a dish in 60 seconds; the "live the same day" activation.
5. **Tips for restaurant owners (10%).** "3 ways to sell more desserts", "How to get more Google reviews" (our Rate
   us button). Useful even for people who don't buy.

**Hooks that work for this product:**
- "Your customers can now *see* this before ordering 👀"
- "This menu has no pictures. It has the actual food."
- "We made this restaurant's menu 3D in 1 minute."
- "POV: you scan the QR and the burger lands on your table."

**Rules:**
- Film real dishes from real (or demo) restaurants. Never stock footage.
- Tag the restaurant every time. Their reshare puts us in front of their local followers, who are exactly our audience.
- Every reel's caption ends with one action: "DM 'MENU' for a free demo at your restaurant".
- Reply to every DM within 2 hours during business hours. A WhatsApp quick-reply template does the first answer.

### 5.2 Local food influencers / bloggers

Micro food bloggers (5k–50k followers, city-specific) are the highest-leverage paid-ish channel at this stage.

- **Barter first:** we make their favourite restaurant's signature dish 3D for free and they post a reel of it. The
  restaurant gets a free month, the blogger gets novel content, we get reach plus a warm intro to the restaurant.
- 4–6 collaborations per city in the first 90 days. Pay ₹2–5k per reel only once barter stops working.
- Track every collaboration with its own demo-request link or code word ("DM 'FOODIE'").

### 5.3 Partnerships (B2B word of mouth)

| Partner | What we offer them | What they give us |
|---|---|---|
| **Restaurant consultants / F&B setup agencies** | 20% of first-year revenue for every restaurant they bring | Introductions to new openings |
| **Menu-card printers & signage shops** | Referral commission; we're a complement, not a threat (standees still get printed) | Their walk-in restaurant clients |
| **POS / billing software resellers** (local Petpooja / similar dealers) | Referral commission | Access to their restaurant base |
| **Restaurant associations** (local NRAI chapter, city hotel & restaurant associations) | A free talk/demo at their meet; a member discount | A room full of owners |
| **Hospitality & hotel-management colleges** | Free workshop on AR in F&B | Students as part-time capture reps; goodwill |
| Interior designers for cafés | Referral commission | New openings |

### 5.4 Website & search (slow, but compounding)

- Replace the ReCapture **waitlist** with **"Book a free demo"** (name, restaurant, area, phone) once the Play
  listing is public. Route each lead to the city's rep on WhatsApp.
- **One landing page per city:** "3D QR Menu for Restaurants in <City>". Use local terms and show live examples from that city.
- **A demo menu link on the homepage**: "Scan this to try it". The product is its own best page.
- Blog only what owners actually search for: "QR menu for restaurant", "digital menu card price", "how to get more
  Google reviews for restaurant", "restaurant offer ideas". Two posts a month at most. This is a 6–12 month payoff.
- List MayasabhaXR on Google Business Profile, JustDial and IndiaMART ("digital menu", "QR menu").

### 5.5 Paid ads (only after proof, see §8 for budget)

**Don't run ads before** (a) the public Play listing and demo-booking page exist and (b) reps convert at least 1 in 5 demos. Ads only multiply a funnel that already works.

When ready:

| Channel | Targeting | Creative | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Meta (Instagram + FB) local ads** | 3–5 km radius around the target clusters; interests: restaurant owner, café, food business, hospitality; age 24–50 | The best-performing organic reel (boost what already works, never make ad-only creative) | WhatsApp click-to-chat → rep books a visit |
| **Google Search** | "QR menu for restaurant", "digital menu card", "3D menu", "<city> + menu QR" | Text ads → city landing page | Demo form |
| **Retargeting** | People who watched 50%+ of a reel or visited the site | Owner testimonial reel + "30 days free" | Demo form / WhatsApp |

**Measure:** cost per demo booked and cost per **paid** restaurant, not clicks or likes. Kill any ad set whose cost
per booked demo goes above ₹500 after ₹3,000 of spend.

## 6. Activation: lead → live restaurant

Our best asset is **same-day activation**. Protect it.

1. **Lead arrives** (DM, form, referral) → WhatsApp reply within 2 hours → visit booked within 48 hours.
2. **Visit** → the 10-minute pitch (§4.2) → standee activated → **free 30-day trial** started by the rep.
3. **Day 0–2:** the rep adds at least 8 dishes, with **at least 3 in 3D** (the wow), plus logo, cover and hours,
   then publishes. A menu with 2 dishes and no photos is worse than no menu.
4. **Day 3:** the rep calls: "Did customers scan? Show me." Fix anything that isn't working.
5. **Day 7:** share the restaurant's first numbers from Analytics on WhatsApp ("43 people opened your menu, 12 saw the
   paneer in AR").
6. **Day 20–25:** convert the trial. Use the in-app "Pay now" / autopay. The rep visits in person for anyone who
   hasn't paid by day 25.

**Activation metric:** % of trials that reach "8+ dishes, 3+ in 3D, published" within 3 days. Target ≥ 80%.

**Before launch, switch on:** `appearanceEnabled` (themes, badges and spotlight are what make demos look premium),
real prices, and weekly reports for live restaurants.

## 7. Retention, word of mouth & referral

### 7.1 Keep them paying

| What | How (already built) |
|---|---|
| Show value every week | The **Monday weekly report** with tips. Make sure owners actually open it; the rep forwards a screenshot in the first month |
| Make daily use easy | **Today screen** (sold out, price change) + **Staff** so the manager does it, not the owner |
| Give them reasons to come back | Festival **offers & happy hour**, **announcement strip** (Diwali, IPL nights), new dishes captured each month |
| Catch churn early | Admin **Subscriptions → Expiring 7d / In grace** list reviewed every Monday. The rep calls each one |
| Upsell | Taste → Signature when they ask for colours, offers or WhatsApp buttons ("held back on your plan" is the natural moment) |
| Rep check-in | Monthly 15-minute visit for the first 3 months: capture 1–2 new dishes in 3D for free |

### 7.2 Word of mouth (diners → owners)

- **Every menu is an ad.** Add a small *"3D menu by Mirage · get one for your restaurant"* link at the bottom of
  every public menu, pointing to the city landing page with a `?ref=<restaurant>` tag. (Product to-do: confirm a
  footer link exists. Add it if not.)
- **Standee design:** keep "Scan to see the dish in 3D" big, and a small Mayasabha mark at the bottom. Diners who
  run a business will notice.
- **"My plate" and AR are shareable.** Encourage restaurants to put a table tent saying "Try the AR, post it, tag us".
  User-generated reels are free reach for the restaurant and for us.

### 7.3 Owner referral program

- **Give 1 month, get 1 month:** when an owner refers another restaurant that becomes paid, both get a free month.
  (Admins can apply this today through the comp / extend tools. Build an in-app referral code later.)
- **Founding Restaurant badge:** the first 30 restaurants per city get a lifetime 20% discount in exchange for a
  testimonial video and permission to use their numbers.
- **Owner WhatsApp group per city** ("Mirage Restaurant Club"): share tips, festival offer ideas and new features.
  Owners recommend tools to each other there.

## 8. Revenue & budget

### 8.1 Pricing for launch

Keep the list prices (₹1,199 / ₹1,799 / ₹2,499, yearly −30%). Don't discount the list price. Use these instead:
- **30-day free trial** (already built), started by the rep.
- **Founding Restaurant offer:** first 30 per city get 20% off for life + priority support.
- **Push yearly** at month 2–3 when the weekly report shows value. Yearly = cash up front + lower churn.
- **Lead with Signature** in pitches. It's the plan where offers, colours, WhatsApp buttons and My plate show up,
  which are the features owners react to in demos.

### 8.2 Unit economics to watch (fill in as data arrives)

| Number | Assumption now | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Average revenue per restaurant | ~₹1,700/month | Most should land on Signature |
| Monthly churn | Target < 4% | Above 6% means fix retention before scaling acquisition |
| Customer acquisition cost (rep salary + incentive + standees + ads ÷ paid restaurants) | Target ≤ ₹5,000 | Pays back in about 3 months |
| Rep productivity | ≥ 15 paid restaurants / rep / month | Below 8 means fix the pitch or the area, not add reps |

### 8.3 Budget tiers (pick one when the budget is decided)

| Tier | Monthly spend (excl. rep salaries) | What it buys |
|---|---|---|
| **A: Bootstrap** | ₹15–25k | Demo standees + printing (₹5k), barter influencer meals (₹5k), freelance reel editor (₹8–10k), tools (₹2k). **No ads.** |
| **B: Proof → growth** | ₹50–80k | Tier A + part-time social media person + paid micro-influencers (₹15k) + Meta local ads test (₹15–20k) + association events |
| **C: Scale** (after 100 paid restaurants and churn < 4%) | ₹1.5–3 lakh | Tier B + steady Meta/Google ads per city (₹1 lakh+) + a full-time marketing lead + a second city team |

**Rule:** move up a tier only when the current one hits its target cost per paid restaurant. Keep 10–15% of any tier for experiments.

## 10. What to measure

**North-star metric:** number of **paying restaurants whose menu was scanned at least 50 times in the last week.**
Paying shows they value it, and 50 scans shows diners actually use it.

**Weekly funnel (one shared Google Sheet; add a CRM later):**

| Stage | Metric | Target (by day 90) |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | Reel views, profile visits, DMs | 50k views/month per city |
| Leads | Demo requests (DM + form + referral + walk-in) | 60 / month per city |
| Demos | Owner actually scanned the demo | 70% of leads |
| Trials | Standee activated + trial started | 30% of demos |
| Activated | 8+ dishes, 3+ in 3D, published in 3 days | 80% of trials |
| Paid | Trial → paid | 40% |
| Retained | Still paying after 90 days | 85% |
| Referral | Restaurants that came from an owner or partner | 20% of new restaurants |

The **source of every lead** (reel, blogger, rep walk-in, partner, menu footer, ad) must be written down. Without
it, we can't tell which channel to spend on.

**Already in the product:** per-restaurant analytics and weekly reports (use them in pitches), Admin →
Subscriptions (trials, expiring, grace), Admin → Published standees (activations per rep).

## 11. Rules, risks and open decisions

### Don't promise (it isn't true yet)

- AR room placement on **iPhone** (iPhone diners get the 3D viewer).
- WhatsApp delivery of the weekly report, or bulk WhatsApp messages to customers.
- Hindi/regional PDF menus. (The printable menu was removed.)
- 3D spin videos.
- Online ordering or payments for diners (we link to Zomato/Swiggy/WhatsApp, we don't take orders).

### Legal / platform rules

- **Google reviews:** never ask only happy customers to review, and never offer anything for reviews. Our "Rate us"
  is built to Google's policy. Keep the marketing copy in line with it.
- **Customer sign-ups (DPDP):** the consent box is never pre-ticked. Don't sell bulk WhatsApp blasts to owners.
- **No fake testimonials,** and no using a restaurant's name or logo in our ads without written permission.

### Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Reps activate trials but don't build good menus → bad first impression | Pay incentive on **paid**, not activated; "8 dishes / 3 in 3D" activation standard |
| Owners say "nice" but don't pay after trial | Day-7 numbers message; rep visit on day 20–25; lead with Signature value |
| A rep-published restaurant that doesn't pay has its page go dark after 7 days, visible to diners | Reps should start the free **trial** (30 days) instead of publishing unpaid wherever possible |
| Spreading thin across 3 cities | One cluster per city at a time; don't open city 3 until city 1 has 30 live |
| Content becomes generic | Every reel features a real dish and a real restaurant |

### Open decisions (founders)

1. **Monthly marketing budget:** choose Tier A, B or C (§8.3).
2. **Which 2–3 cities, and which first cluster in each?**
3. **Rep compensation:** base + per-paid-restaurant incentive amounts.
4. **Founding Restaurant offer:** confirm 20% lifetime for the first 30 per city.
5. **Who owns social media day to day** (in-house person vs. freelancer) and who approves posts.
6. **Menu footer link** ("3D menu by Mirage"): approve adding it to every public menu.
7. **Brand name on social media:** "Mirage Menu" (what restaurants buy) vs. "MayasabhaXR" (the company). Recommendation: social
   handles and ads use **Mirage Menu**. MayasabhaXR is the company behind it.
