================================================================================
RECAPTURE PHASE 2 -- WHAT IT IS, IN ONE PAGE
For anyone new to the project. Read this first; the long docs can wait.
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS

  Phase 1 let someone photograph a real object and get a 3D model back.

  Phase 2 turns those models into a public storefront. The user arranges their
  products in the app, presses Publish once, and gets a permanent QR code.
  Any customer can scan it and browse the products in 3D and AR on their own
  phone.


WHO USES IT

  A small business owner. A cafe, a restaurant, a furniture shop. One phone,
  not technical. They want three things: products that look good, a QR code
  they can print, and proof that someone scanned it.


THE WHOLE IDEA IN ONE PICTURE

    photograph a dish  ->  pick the 3D model  ->  build the catalog
                                                        |
                                                        v
                                                     preview
                                                        |
                                                        v
        analytics  <-  customers scan  <-  QR code  <- PUBLISH


================================================================================
WHAT THE USER CAN DO
================================================================================

SIGN IN
  Phone number, one-time code, in. No password, ever.

CAPTURE A PRODUCT IN 3D
  Walk one circle around it taking 6 photos. About a minute.
  A longer 48-photo mode exists for when quality matters more than time.
  The app rejects blurry, dark and shaky shots as they happen, and corrects
  the user's angle.
  Photos upload in the background. The user can switch apps or lose signal.
  One capture can produce several 3D models. THE USER SEES ALL OF THEM and
  picks the one that actually looks like the dish.

BUILD THE CATALOG
  One catalog per account. Add a name, a logo, business details.
  Sort products into categories: Starters, Mains, Desserts.
  Add a product two ways:
      - from a finished capture, choosing which 3D model it uses
      - as a photo only, for things never worth scanning
  Give it a name, price, description, category.
  Mark items featured, out of stock, or tag them.
  Edit, duplicate, reorder, archive, restore or delete anything, one at a
  time or in bulk.
  ALL OF THIS IS A DRAFT. No customer sees any of it yet.

CHECK BEFORE GOING LIVE
  Preview shows the draft exactly as a customer will see it.
  If anything would break, the app lists EVERY problem at once, each as a
  plain sentence like "'Paneer Tikka' has no 3D model yet." Some rows carry
  the fix as a button beside them.

GO LIVE
  Publish is one button, one deliberate action. It runs in the background.
  Products turn green one at a time. Anything that fails says why, in plain
  English, and Retry pushes only the failures.
  The QR code is generated for them. Download it as PNG or PDF, share it,
  print it.
  Editing anything afterwards raises a "Draft changes not yet live" badge
  until they publish again.
  They can take the catalog offline and the QR code still works.

SEE WHAT HAPPENED
  Page views, visitors, product views, AR launches, contact taps.
  A chart by day, a ranked list of top products, and a comparison against the
  previous period. The user turned no tracking on. It comes with publishing.


================================================================================
HOW IT GOES IN PRACTICE -- ONE AFTERNOON
================================================================================

  1.  Signs in with a phone number.                          5 seconds

  2.  Fills in business name, phone, address. Uploads a logo. 2 minutes

  3.  Photographs seven dishes, one circle of six photos
      each. Uploads run in the background while they shoot
      the next one.                                          10 minutes

  4.  Creates three categories. Adds each dish as a product,
      choosing which of its 3D models to use. Adds two
      drinks as photo-only products.                         10 minutes

  5.  Opens Preview, spots a dark photo, replaces it.        1 minute

  6.  Presses Publish. The app stops them with two problems
      to fix. Fixes both, publishes again. Two products fail
      on a bad connection; Retry pushes just those two.      3 minutes

  7.  Downloads the QR PDF, sends it to a print shop, opens
      the link on their own phone to check. The dishes spin.
      AR puts a plate on their actual table.                 1 minute

  8.  Two weeks later they change four prices and publish
      again. THE PRINTED QR ON EVERY TABLE KEEPS WORKING.

  9.  Three weeks later: 340 views, 210 visitors, 61 people
      launched AR. They go photograph four more dishes.


================================================================================
THE FOUR RULES THE DESIGN RESTS ON
================================================================================

  1. NOTHING IS LIVE UNTIL PUBLISH IS PRESSED.
     Editing a live catalog is always safe. Customers keep seeing the last
     published version until the user says otherwise.

  2. THE QR CODE NEVER CHANGES.
     Rename the business, change every price, republish fifty times, take the
     catalog offline and bring it back. The printed sticker keeps working.
     This is what makes it safe to print on day one.

  3. A HALF-FINISHED PUBLISH SAYS WHICH HALF.
     Every product carries its own status and its own reason. Retry pushes
     only what failed, never all of it again.

  4. NO TECHNICAL ERROR EVER REACHES THE SCREEN.
     Every failure is translated into a sentence with a next step.


================================================================================
WHAT IT CANNOT DO YET
================================================================================

Limits of the public catalog platform, not of the app. Do not promise these.

  - Out of stock is private. It filters the owner's list, not the public page.
  - Tags are private. Customers never see them.
  - Website and social links are stored but do not appear publicly.
  - Drag order inside the app does not fully carry to the public page.
  - iPhone AR room placement is not served yet. iPhone customers get the 3D
    viewer instead.
  - Converting a photo-only product to 3D restarts that product's analytics.


================================================================================
WHAT IS COMING NEXT
================================================================================

Today the business owner does every step themselves.

Next is SAME-DAY ACTIVATION: a sales rep walks in with a box of pre-printed QR
standees, does the whole job for the owner in one visit, sticks a standee on
the table and leaves with the catalog live. Dishes go live as photos that
afternoon and quietly become 3D hours later when generation finishes.

For the owner, the whole thing collapses into "someone came, took photos of my
food, and left me a QR code that works."

Designed, not built yet.
