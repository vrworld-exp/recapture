SAME-DAY ACTIVATION — SALES REP FIELD GUIDE
===========================================
One visit: close the deal, shoot the dishes, leave a working QR standee behind.
About 15 minutes per restaurant. Keep this open the first few times.

BEFORE YOU LEAVE THE OFFICE
---------------------------
1.  Your account needs the SALES_REP role. There is no button for it — ask
    engineering.
2.  Take printed standees. Each has an 8-character code under the QR square.
3.  Take unused standees only. A used one is refused at step 8.
4.  Charge your phone. The dish capture is the battery-hungry part.

STEP 1 — SIGN IN
----------------
5.  Open the app on your phone, or the web build in Chrome. Both work.
6.  Enter your phone number, tap Send code.
7.  Type the 6-digit OTP. (On dev builds the code is 555555.)
    You land on your rep home — the restaurants you may act on. Empty at first.

STEP 2 — ACTIVATE THE STANDEE
-----------------------------
8.  Tap "Activate a standee" and type the 8-character code.
      - Case, hyphens and spaces do not matter.
      - You can paste the whole link if you scanned it with your phone camera.
      - I, L, O and U are never used in a code. If you see one it is a 1 or 0.
9.  Tap Continue. The code is checked BEFORE you are asked for anything else,
    so you never fill in a restaurant's details against a dead standee.
      "already in use"            -> use a different standee.
      "not one of ours"           -> re-read the characters.
      "restaurant already live"   -> that standee cannot be moved; use a fresh one.
10. Enter the restaurant's NAME and PHONE NUMBER.

    *** THE PHONE NUMBER IS THE MOST IMPORTANT THING YOU TYPE TODAY. ***
    It becomes the owner's sign-in identity and cannot be edited afterwards.
    A wrong digit shows no error anywhere. It silently creates an account for
    a number nobody owns, and the real owner can never get in. Fixing it needs
    an engineer running a repair script.

11. Tap Continue. The app reads the number back with the country code.
    READ IT ALOUD AND HAVE THE OWNER CONFIRM IT. This screen exists only for
    that. Do not tap past it.
12. Tap Activate. The standee is now bound to this restaurant, permanently.
    You will see the live link.

STEP 3 — ADD THE DISHES
-----------------------
13. From your rep home, open the restaurant you just activated.
14. Tap "Add a dish". Three sources, on the phone AND in the browser:
      Capture now   - shoot the dish for AR.
      3D model      - use a capture you finished earlier.
      Photo         - a plain photo. No AR, but instant.
15. "Capture now" works differently on the two, and it matters:

    ON THE PHONE - the guided six-photo ring, as always. The app tracks where
    you are standing and will not accept two shots from the same spot. Follow
    the ring; it tells you when a wedge is done.

    IN THE BROWSER - six photos you take yourself, one tap each. A laptop has
    no motion sensor, so NOTHING CHECKS YOUR ANGLE. That is the whole
    difference, and it is on you to cover it:
      - Name the dish BEFORE you start. The browser asks first, because the
        capture is saved under that name and you will need to find it after.
      - Move about a sixth of the way around the dish between every shot.
        Six evenly spread shots make a model; six from the same chair do not.
      - Keep the whole dish in frame each time.
      - "Retake last" drops only the shot you just took.
    The model is built from the best of your six either way.

16. When the capture comes back, pick it in the list and name the dish.
17. Names must be unique within one restaurant. Use what a diner would read.
18. Repeat per dish. There is no bulk import.
19. Watch each row's badge:
      "3D generating..."  model being built. Minutes, not seconds.
      "AR ready"          that dish now has a working AR button.
      "3D unavailable"    the model failed. The dish still works in 2D.
      "Photo only"        an image-only dish. Expected, not an error.
    You do not have to wait — it keeps working after you close the app.

STEP 4 — BEFORE YOU LEAVE THE TABLE
-----------------------------------
20. *** THE MENU IS NOT LIVE YET. *** This is the part that surprises people.
    Activating binds the standee. It does not put the menu online. Scanning it
    right now shows a "not live yet" page, which is correct.

    The menu goes live when EITHER:
      (a) a 3D dish finishes generating — that publishes automatically; or
      (b) the owner signs in and taps Publish.

    So if you added ONLY photo dishes, nothing publishes on its own. Tell the
    owner plainly: they must sign in and publish, or the standee stays dead.
21. Have the owner sign in NOW, on their own phone, with the number you typed.
    Best use of your last five minutes: it proves the number was right while
    you can still fix it, it lets them publish, and if they cannot get in you
    are still in the room.
22. Scan the standee yourself and check where it lands.
23. Leave the standee on the table.

WHAT WORKS WHERE
----------------
24. Phone and browser now do the SAME THINGS. Nothing is missing on either:
      Sign in / activate / type a code ......... phone YES   browser YES
      Scan a standee in the app ............... phone YES   browser YES
      See your restaurants .................... phone YES   browser YES
      Add a photo or finished-capture dish .... phone YES   browser YES
      SHOOT a dish ............................ phone YES   browser YES
25. The one real difference is HOW a dish gets shot, not whether it can be:
      Phone   - guided ring. It knows where you are standing and refuses a
                second shot from the same wedge. It catches your mistakes.
      Browser - six manual shots. No motion sensor exists on a laptop, so
                nothing checks your spacing. It catches nothing.
    Both end in the same 3D model. Use the phone at a table if you have it;
    the browser is there so a desk visit or a dead phone is not a lost menu.
26. The in-app scanner needs a camera and a secure page. If the browser says
    it cannot open one, you are probably on an http:// address — or you denied
    the camera. Typing the 8 characters always works and is never wrong.
27. About 30 activations per hour. Normal visits never reach this.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
28. Wrong phone number typed -> escalate to engineering IMMEDIATELY with the
    restaurant name and the correct number. Do NOT re-activate.
29. Standee lost or damaged -> attach a fresh code to the same restaurant. The
    old link keeps working until it is retired separately.
30. "You can no longer edit this restaurant" -> your access was revoked; ask
    for it again.
31. Dish stuck on "3D generating" over an hour -> report it. It still shows 2D.
32. Anything you cannot explain to the owner -> note the restaurant name, the
    standee code and the time, and send it on. Do not guess.

THE ONE-LINE VERSION
--------------------
33. Code in -> read the phone number aloud -> shoot the dishes -> have the
    owner sign in and publish before you leave.



New how to use this ------------------------------>>>>>>>>>>

What the rep does
1. Get to the rep area
Open app → Profile → tap My restaurants (the row I just added).

2. Start an activation
Tap the Activate a standee button (bottom right).

3. Type the code
Every printed standee has an 8-character code under the QR square — like ABCD2345. Type it. You can also paste the whole link if you scanned with your phone's normal camera.

In Chrome there is no "Scan the code" button. That's on purpose — the browser can't scan, so the button is hidden rather than shown broken.

4. Tap Continue
The app checks that code is still free before asking anything else — so the rep never fills in a full form against a standee someone already took.

5. Enter the restaurant's details
Name + phone number. ⚠️ That phone number becomes the restaurant owner's login. Type it wrong and the owner can't get into their own account.

6. Confirm
The app reads the number back. The rep taps to confirm. This step exists so a typo is caught at the table, not by a support call later.

7. Done
You get the live link and the phone number shown again. Then tap Add dishes.

8. Add each dish
Tap Add a dish, then pick where it comes from:

Source	Phone	Chrome
Capture now (shoot it)	✅	❌ hidden
From a finished capture	✅	✅
Photo only (no AR)	✅	✅
9. Watch it turn ready
The dish shows "3D generating…", then flips to "AR ready" by itself — no pull-to-refresh. Seeing that flip is the point of the visit; it's how the rep knows it worked before leaving.
